# IMDM101-Assignment-4

We created a simple game where you play as a hungry monkey that is looking for moving bananas to collect across 3 different scenes. A city, a forest, and an underwater ocean environment. In the city and forest levels, the player can freely move around terrain to catch moving bananas, and they're moving in such a way as to add a little bit of challenge for the player. The underwater scene increases the difficulty a little bit, since the bananas spawn behind sand dunes which act as obstacles that the player has to move around in order to collect the bananas.
