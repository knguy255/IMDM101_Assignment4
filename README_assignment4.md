# IMDM101-Assignment-4

We created a simple game where you play as a monkey collecting bananas in different scenes
a city, an underwater scene, and a jungle (forest).
- The city and forest scenes spawn bananas.
- The underwater scene spawns bananas behind the sand dunes and you have to avoid them or you won't be able to collect the bananas
